# Epic card art briefs (v2.9)

Seven paintings for the new Epic cards. Save each as `art/<id>.webp`, **480 × 360 px (landscape, 4:3)**, the same size
as the other non-Legendary cards. Until a file exists, the game shows a procedural stand-in.

**Shared style (matches the current cards):** dark, densely detailed, hand-drawn look, fine etched linework and
crosshatching over muted painterly colour, like an engraving with ink washes. Almost black backgrounds; one accent light per
faction. Grim and mature, never cartoonish. No text, logos, borders or frames in the image. The subject fills the frame and
reads at thumbnail size; keep the bottom fifth calm (the card's name plate covers it).

Faction palettes:
- **The Abyss:** drowned blacks and deep teal, a sickly green-yellow glow (bioluminescence, eyes).
- **The Derelict:** rusted bronze, gunmetal and grime; cold blue rim light and one hot orange or red sensor light.
- **The Red Communion:** deep crimson, candlelight, wet flesh, black lace and bone.
- **Wanderers:** tarnished brass, torchlight, worn canvas and grimy faces in the dark.

---

## v45 · The Hadal Cantor (The Abyss, Epic)
A towering eyeless figure standing waist-deep in black water inside a flooded cathedral nave, its long throat split into
dozens of thin vertical slits that glow faint green as though singing. Drowned worshippers float face-down around it in a
loose circle; their open mouths trail threads of luminous green that rise and twist into the Cantor's throat. Broken pews
and pillars recede into darkness. Mood: a silent, deafening hymn.

## v46 · Leviathan of the Lightless Trench (The Abyss, Epic)
Looking down a sheer undersea canyon into absolute black, where the canyon walls themselves are revealed to be the
ridged, barnacled flank of something impossibly large. One enormous half-lidded eye has just opened in the trench floor,
glowing green-yellow and lighting drifting silt. A tiny diver with a lamp hangs in the foreground for scale, frozen.
Mood: a mouth miles wide, beginning to open.

## m45 · Foundry-Ship KESTREL (The Derelict, Epic)
A colossal derelict factory-ship drifting in space, its hull open like a ribcage to show an automated assembly line still
running inside: robotic arms welding new drones under sputtering orange sparks. A stream of small spider-legged drones
pours out of a launch bay into the dark. Hull lettering is worn and unreadable; cold blue starlight on rusted plating.
Mood: the crew is long dead, and the machine keeps building.

## m46 · Hull-Link Warden (The Derelict, Epic)
A squat, heavily armoured guardian construct braced in a narrow ship corridor, its thick magnetic clamp-arms locked into
the bulkheads on both sides, joined to two smaller drones beside it by heavy cables and glowing blue linkage. One red
sensor eye in a scarred faceplate. Sparks, steam and scorch marks on the walls; something unseen has been hammering at it.
Mood: an immovable wall that protects everything beside it.

## c45 · Abbess of Open Wounds (The Red Communion, Epic)
A tall abbess in a blood-soaked crimson habit with black lace and a bone-white veil, arms held wide in blessing. Her palms
and forearms are covered in neat, open, ritual cuts, each releasing a slow thread of blood that flows through the air
into the mouths of kneeling cultists below her. Rows of candles and a dark altar behind. Her face is calm and serene;
the cultists look ecstatic. Mood: holy, generous, horrifying.

## c46 · Mother of the Red Litter (The Red Communion, Epic)
A hunched matriarch in a tattered red shawl seated on a throne of bones and rags, cradling a mass of small, pale,
many-eyed Spawnlings in her lap and at her feet; more crawl from the folds of her skirts. A severed rope and a sacrificial
knife lie on the floor before her. Dim candlelight and wet red reflections. Her expression is tender and proud.
Mood: every sacrifice comes back smaller and hungrier.

## n17 · The Last Quartermaster (Wanderers, Epic)
A weathered woman in a patched brass-buttoned coat standing at a makeshift desk in a dim cargo bay, holding up a lantern
over two open ledgers: one with names neatly crossed out, one still being written. Behind her, a handful of ragged
survivors with salvaged weapons gather close in the lantern light; a sealed bulkhead door looms in the darkness.
Mood: grim, protective, the last steady hand.
